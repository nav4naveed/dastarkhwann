Our Story

A family table, open to the whole city

Karachi Dastarkhwan began as a small nihari stall near Burns Road, run by one family and a single copper deg. Word spread, the queues grew, and the stall became the restaurant you know today.

We still start our nihari before dawn, grind our own spices every morning, and grill every kebab over real charcoal. A dastarkhwan is the cloth where family and guests share a meal — and that is exactly how we want you to feel here.
